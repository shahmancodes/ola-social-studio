# Native design recipe credits

Ola adapts the eight layouts approved in the comparison gallery into its measured scene renderer. These are format-specific geometry adaptations, with new layout, fitting, containment, editing and validation code. No upstream sample copy, logos, photographs, decorative asset packs or source font files are included in generation. Brand assets and fonts come from the company snapshot.
